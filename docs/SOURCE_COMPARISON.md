Targeted comparison found no direct import at these locators of the stated
prime-family exact cost or the full original-coordinate comparison. This is
not proof of novelty. The contribution is these precise arithmetic statements
and certificates; CRT, integer algebra, rounding, cyclotomic valuation and
positive Fourier inversion are classical ingredients.

Recent primary sources were also screened at specific statements:
[Pasten's largest-prime-factor note](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.01327v1),
Theorem1.1 and Corollaries1.2–1.3, gives a different pointwise hybrid bound;
[Matala-aho](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.20028v1), Proposition1, treats fixed pairs;
[Pascadi's Kloosterman paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00039-026-00746-0),
Theorems1.1–1.2 and Corollary1.6, concerns different spectral objects.
Those inspected statements do not supply the growing-dimensional,
same-input phase returns needed here. No exhaustive literature clearance,
specialist consultation or priority determination is claimed.
