# Known limits

Unbounded primitive/useful separation has not been proved inside the family
2+3^k, nor over radical-small triples. The variable-base comparison needs an
actual infinite sequence with a common index that simultaneously makes drift
and every relevant phase small relative to the primitive cost.

The sparse-certificate obstruction is in density, at feasible B<=x^theta with
fixed theta<2, and applies to fixed-order or polynomially many individually
displayed positive Fourier terms. It does not exclude returns, rare bases,
larger o(G) budgets or compressed collective frequency sums. No positive density
of the feasible branch is asserted.

The prime-family theorem supplies an unconditional unbounded ratio but all
those triples have rad(abc)>c. Growing primitive costs alone are not an unbounded
ratio. A finite record is not an infinite family.

Source comparisons are bounded and do not establish priority. The weighted-radius
formula is retained as a proved geometric proposition without a novelty claim.
The analytic input and its numerical sieve integrals are attributed, not
independently recomputed. AI review is not specialist acceptance. No abc result
or certified formal proof is asserted. Independent specialist review remains
outstanding.
